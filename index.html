Setup (5 minutes) / सेटअप

  1. Same Wi‑Fi: TV aur phone dono ek hi Wi‑Fi par hon. (दोनों एक ही वाई‑फ़ाई पर)
  2. TV: Android TV par ek browser kholo (Play Store se TV Bro ya Chrome), ye website kholo aur “This is the TV” chuno. Screen par QR code aur 4‑digit code aayega.
  3. Phone: OnePlus ke Chrome mein QR scan karo (ya site kholke “This is the phone camera” dabao aur code daalo). Camera allow karo.
  4. Phone rakho: landscape (आड़ा), kamar ki unchai par, TV ke paas, aapki taraf. 2–3 meter peeche khade ho – sir se pair tak dikhna chahiye. Phone charger par rakho.
  5. Khelo: TV par game chunne ke liye haath upar karke game ke upar 1–2 second rakho, ya TV remote ke arrow + OK button use karo. Back button = menu.

If the TV browser gives trouble / अगर टीवी ब्राउज़र न चले

Option A – Cast: Phone par “One device mode” kholo, phone ko stand par player ki taraf rakho, aur phone ki screen TV par Cast / Screen mirror karo (Quick settings → Cast / Smart View).

Option B – Laptop: Laptop ko HDMI se TV se jodo, laptop par “This is the TV” kholo (phone camera ke saath), ya “One device mode” (laptop webcam ke saath).

The 12 games / 12 खेल

🏃 Jungle Dash – endless runner
🍉 Slice Storm – cut fruit
🧱 Shape Gate – pose through the wall
🤸 Jack Attack – jumping jacks
🏗️ Squat Tower – squats
🫧 Bubble Pop – reach & pop
🧤 Goal Guard – goalkeeper
🥊 Punch Pads – boxing
🐦 Sky Glider – flap your arms
☄️ Meteor Dodge – dodge with body
🧘 Yoga Freeze – hold poses
🏁 Sprint Race – run in place
💃 Dance Studio – add a YouTube song (send the link from the phone page), ● Record the steps once by dancing with the video, then ▶ Practice: copy the white shadow dancer and get PERFECT/GREAT/OK/MISS per move, with slow‑speed practice. गाने के स्टेप्स सीखो।

All games work for 1 player; a 2nd person can step in next to you for 2‑player mode. Pose tracking runs on the phone; only body points (not video) are sent to the TV.
