Tips / सुझाव
  • Phone ko landscape (आड़ा) rakho, kamar ki height par, TV ke neeche ya paas.
  • Player 2–3 meter door khade ho. Sir se pair tak poora sharir dikhna chahiye.
  • Push-ups / plank: phone ko neeche, aapke side mein rakho.
  • Achhi roshni (light) rakho, peeche se tez light na ho. Charger laga ke rakho.
  • TV par button chunne ke liye: haath uthao aur button par 1–2 second rakho.
